———×———# Disciplinary Disparities for Students with Disabilities
## A Case Study Using CRDC 2020-21 Data

## Executive Summary

This case study analyzes data from the U.S. Department of Education Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) to quantify how students with disabilities experience exclusionary discipline at disproportionate rates. Drawing on the 2020-21 CRDC release, the analysis covers national, Texas state, and local district levels.

**Central findings:**
- Students with disabilities (SWD) are 1.99x more likely to receive an out-of-school suspension
- SWD are 2.70x more likely to receive multiple OOS suspensions
- Black SWD face the highest absolute rate at 3.46%
- Pflugerville ISD suspends SWD at nearly 11% vs 2.22% nationally
- Discipline disparities widened between 2017-18 and 2020-21

## Data Source

**Source:** CRDC 2020-21 (U.S. Dept. of Education, released 2023)
**Measures:** In-School Suspension, OOS Suspension, Referral to Law Enforcement, School Arrest, Physical Restraint, Mechanical Restraint, Seclusion
**Disparity ratio** = SWD rate / non-SWD rate

## Finding 1: National Disparities

| Measure | SWD Rate | Non-SWD Rate | Ratio |
|---------|----------|--------------|-------|
| In-School Suspension | 2.22% | 1.48% | 1.50x |
| OOS Suspension (1+) | 2.22% | 1.12% | 1.99x |
| OOS Suspension (multiple) | 0.76% | 0.28% | 2.70x |
| Referral to Law Enforcement | 0.21% | 0.11% | 1.86x |
| School Arrest | 0.03% | 0.02% | 1.89x |

## Finding 2: Race x Disability

| Race | SWD Rate | Non-SWD Rate | Ratio |
|------|----------|--------------|-------|
| Black | 3.46% | 2.01% | 1.72x |
| White | 2.39% | 1.18% | 2.03x |
| Hispanic | 1.23% | 0.70% | 1.76x |
| Asian | 0.38% | 0.16% | 2.42x |
| Am. Indian | 2.71% | 1.74% | 1.56x |

## Finding 3: Texas and District Comparison

| Geography | SWD OOS Rate | Non-SWD Rate | Ratio |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|-------|
| National | 2.22% | 1.12% | 1.99x |
| Texas | 3.72% | 1.43% | 2.60x |
| Pflugerville ISD | 10.99% | 3.52% | 3.12x |
| Austin ISD | 0.36% | 0.09% | 4.11x |

## Finding 4: Restraint and Seclusion

Physical restraint is concentrated 81% on IDEA students nationally (84% in Texas). Texas seclusion rates are very low due to Texas Education Code Section 37.0021 restricting its use.

## Finding 5: Trend 2017-18 vs 2020-21

Absolute discipline counts dropped due to COVID-19 remote schooling, but disparity ratios held or widened:
- Multiple OOS suspension ratio widened from 2.28x to 2.70x
- Students with disabilities bore a larger share of remaining discipline

## Implications

1. Disproportionality is systemic across all measures, geographies, and racial groups
2. Manifestation determination reviews are insufficient to protect SWD
3. Physical restraint is default response when proactive supports are absent
4. Intersectional risk (race + disability) compounds disparities significantly
5. District-level policy choices matter: Pflugerville vs Round Rock show a 3x difference
